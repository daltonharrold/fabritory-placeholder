Fabritory prints parts with the color already in them, and the soft parts too. Rigid and flexible fuse in a single print, so nothing needs painting, gluing or bolting together afterwards.

Color goes in, not on.

Cut a Fabritory part in half and the color is still there. It was printed with the shape, layer by layer, instead of sprayed over the top of it.

  • Soft skin, printed in placeGrips, seals and gaskets are printed as part of the part. Nothing is slipped on or glued down after.
  • Rigid core where the load goesStiff material sits exactly where the part is loaded, fused to the soft material around it.
  • Color that survives a scratchEvery layer carries its own color, so a scuff shows more of the same part, not primer.
