Multicolor · multimaterial 3D printing

One print.
Many colors.

Fabritory makes parts the way you actually picture them: color laid down layer by layer with the shape, rigid and flexible materials fused in the same print. No painting. No gluing. No assembling five pieces into one.

What it does

Color and material, designed in from the first layer.

01 · Multicolor

Color goes in, not on.

Every layer can carry its own color, so logos, labels and full color schemes come off the printer finished. Nothing to paint, nothing to chip.

02 · Multimaterial

Hard and soft, together.

Rigid where it needs strength, flexible where it needs to grip, seal or bend — fused into a single part instead of stuck together after.

03 · Fewer parts

Print it whole.

Hinges, gaskets, grips and housings that used to be separate pieces come out as one. Less assembly, fewer seams, fewer things to go wrong.

04 · Looks like the real thing

Prototypes that look like product.

Show the real colors and the real feel on day one — not a gray mockup with a note that says “imagine it in blue.”

Color and material used to be something you added afterwards. Now they're part of the print.
